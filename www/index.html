How to play

Tap timing (default)

The gun slides up and down the rail on the right by itself. Tap anywhere at the right moment to fire straight ahead. Line the shot up with a gap in the post and a target, and time it. Bows and slingshots fire at a fixed arc, so the timing is all that matters.

Drag to aim (optional)

Switch in Settings. Touch anywhere and drag away from the target like a slingshot: pull right to shoot left, pull down to shoot higher. Release to fire. Drag back to the start point to cancel.

Precision

Guns fire fast but still drop a little. Bows, slingshots and knives arc, and the pull length sets the power. Wind pushes slow projectiles. The sniper rifle sways, so time your release.

The post

Shots have to pass through the gaps in the wooden post. Gaps get narrower, glass panes appear, planks slide and bars spin as you climb.

Scoring

Clear every target within the shot limit. Fewer shots means more stars: three for a perfect run, two for beating par.

Unlimited levels

Every level is generated from a code. Endless mode never runs out, the daily challenge is the same for everyone, and any code can be shared and replayed.

Stuck?

The hint button costs one shot and briefly shows a working trajectory, plus a marker on the rail showing where to fire.
